YuE2 Score Studio

View, play, rearrange and edit the score behind a YuE2 song, then send it back for a re-render.

Drop a score PNG here …or click to choose. The noise-looking image from music-yue2 / cover-yue2. Also accepts .json or .abc.
  1. Load the score PNG a YuE2 job returned. Drop the MP3 too if you want to listen alongside.
  2. Edit: reorder or duplicate sections, make one instrumental, transpose, change tempo, rewrite lyrics or style.
  3. Download the new score PNG and send it to /wf /run:score-yue2 as a file.
Send PNGs as files, not photos. Chat apps recompress photos, which destroys the data hidden in the pixels. Download the original from the job page, and attach it as a file or document when sending it back.

Score

The player is a piano sketch of the score (melody + chords), not YuE2's singing. The re-render performs it with your style.
